Research

The Violent and Transient Universe

Research Interests

What I Study

I am interested in the most energetic and rapidly changing events in the Universe — phenomena that reveal how stars live and die, how compact objects interact, and how extreme physical conditions produce the elements and radiation we observe.

Stellar Explosions & Nucleosynthesis

How stars end their lives and forge the elements through explosive burning and neutrino-driven processes.

Black Holes, Neutron Stars & Accretion

The physics of compact objects, accretion discs, and high-energy emission around them.

Mergers, Disruptions & Transients

Compact-object mergers, tidal disruption events, and the transient sky across wavelengths.

I hope to pursue these areas through a combination of theoretical modelling, computational methods, and observations. My background in computational physics and chemistry gives me a unique advantage in exploring r-process nucleosynthesis and the formation of heavy elements in the violent Universe.
