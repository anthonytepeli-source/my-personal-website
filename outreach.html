Observation & Astronomy

As a research scholar with Prof. P. V. K. Rao at the University of Dodoma, I worked in observational astronomy, including telescope assembly and observations of the Moon and planetary surfaces. I applied imaging polarimetry to material analysis, and later trained at the IRAM 30-meter School on Millimetre Astronomy in interferometry, data calibration, and radio-astronomical instrumentation. I have also learned from Dr. Noorali Jiwaji through astronomy activities and space-science initiatives organized through American Spaces in Tanzania under the U.S. Embassy.

Outreach & Community

In 2022, I volunteered as a physics tutor in village schools, reaching more than 5,000 students. In 2026, through the Rotaract Club of e-Swahili, I served at Rotary-supported medical camps that provided free healthcare to more than 4,000 people in Dar es Salaam. I am also an active member of Youth of the United Nations, engaging in youth-focused global initiatives and community development work.
